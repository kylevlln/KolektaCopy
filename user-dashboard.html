Waste Sorting Reference Guide

Proper segregation saves landfill space and avoids uncollected penalties

🍃 Biodegradable (Nabubulok)

Put out on Monday mornings.

  • Leftover cooked food & rice
  • Fruit & vegetable peels
  • Coffee grounds & tea bags
  • Dry leaves, grass & garden trimmings
  • Eggshells and bones
📦 Non-Biodegradable (Di-Nabubulok)

Put out on Wednesday mornings.

  • Candy wrappers & sachets
  • Dirty plastic bags & styrofoam
  • Sanitary napkins & diapers
  • Worn out shoes & rags
  • Broken ceramics & mirrors
♻️ Recyclable (Naireresiklo)

Put out on Friday mornings.

  • Clean cardboard & newspapers
  • PET water bottles & hard plastics
  • Aluminum soda cans & tin cans
  • Clean glass jars and bottles
  • Scrap metal and wires
